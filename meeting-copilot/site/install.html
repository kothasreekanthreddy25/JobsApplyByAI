Install guide

About five minutes. You need Windows 10 (version 2004 or later) or Windows 11, an internet connection, and accounts with two AI services (set up in Part 2; you pay them directly for what you use).

Part 1: Install

  1. Download the installer Get Meeting Copilot Setup.exe from the download page. It is about 150 MB because it includes the small on-device model that matches your questions to your prep pack.

    Optional: check the file is genuine

    Open PowerShell (Start menu, type powershell) and run: 
    Get-FileHash "$env:USERPROFILE\Downloads\Meeting Copilot Setup.exe" -Algorithm SHA256
    The long code it prints must match the SHA-256 on the download page. If it doesn't, don't run the file and tell us.
  2. Run it. Windows may show a warning once. Double-click the installer. Because the app is new and isn't code-signed yet, Windows SmartScreen will probably show a blue "Windows protected your PC" screen. This is normal for any new unsigned program and is not a sign of a problem with the file (see the checksum above if you want to be sure).

    2. Click Run anyway. The installer then runs in a few seconds and creates a Desktop and Start Menu shortcut. It installs only for you, so no administrator password is needed.
    If there is no "Run anyway" button. Some Windows 11 PCs have Smart App Control turned on, which blocks unsigned apps with no override. Your options: turn Smart App Control off in Windows Security > App & browser control > Smart App Control settings (note that Windows doesn't let you turn it back on without resetting the PC), or wait for the signed version. Only change this if you are comfortable with that trade-off.
    Antivirus alert? Unsigned new programs sometimes trigger false alarms. Check the SHA-256 above. If it matches, you can allow the file in your antivirus, or report the false positive to its vendor.

Part 2: Set up (first launch)

The Settings window opens by itself the first time. You can reopen it any time from the tray icon (bottom-right of the taskbar; click the arrow if it is hidden).

  1. Create your two AI accounts and keys The app uses two services that bill you directly for what you use, usually a few cents for an hour of audio and answers. Nothing is billed through us.
    ServiceWhat it doesHow to get the key
    AnthropicWrites the answersSign up at console.anthropic.com. Add a small amount of credit under Billing, then API keys > Create key.
    DeepgramTurns speech into textSign up at console.deepgram.com (new accounts often include starter credit), then API Keys > Create a New API Key.
    OpenAI optionalBackup onlyNot needed. Skip it.
    Treat keys like passwords. Never post them in chat, email or screenshots. If one leaks, delete it in that service's console and create a new one.
  2. Paste the keys into Settings Paste them into the boxes and click Save and test keys. You should see a green tick for each service. Typical messages: key rejected (a typo or a deleted key), no credit on the account (add credit in the Anthropic console). The keys are stored encrypted on your computer and never sent to us.
  3. Create your prep pack recommended for interview practice In Interview prep pack, click Choose resume (PDF, Word or text; a scanned image won't work), paste the job description, give the pack a name and click Create prep pack. After about a minute you'll see how many questions were made. A readable study sheet (.md) is saved next to it; open it with Open prep folder and read it before you rely on it, because the answers are drafted from your resume and you should check them. During practice, close matches to a prepared question appear instantly (the overlay says "prepared"); other questions are answered by the AI in about a second, using your resume as background.
  4. Add your own study material optional Click Open documents folder and drop in PDF, Word, text or Markdown files (notes, product docs, textbooks). A file inside a sub-folder is used only when the meeting title contains the folder name, for example acme for a call titled "Acme renewal".

Part 3: Use it

  1. Start a session Press Ctrl+Shift+S (or use the tray menu) to start, and again to stop. It also starts by itself when it detects a Zoom, Teams, Google Meet or Webex meeting window. The overlay appears at the top-centre of your screen, just under your webcam.
    Test it first. Start a session and play a video where someone asks a question, or ask one out loud near your speakers. The app listens to what your computer plays, not your microphone, so it hears the other people on a call but not you. Do a test before any real call.
  2. Move and resize the overlay Press Ctrl+Shift+M to unlock it. A dashed frame appears: drag the top bar to move it, drag the bottom-right corner to resize, and press Ctrl+Shift+M again to lock it so clicks pass through. Position, size, transparency and text size are remembered.
  3. Handy keys
    Ctrl+Shift+SStart / stop a session
    Ctrl+Shift+HShow / hide the overlay
    Ctrl+Shift+MUnlock to drag / resize, press again to lock
    Ctrl+Alt + arrow keysMove the overlay (add Shift to resize), works while locked
    Ctrl+Alt+[ / ]Background more / less transparent
    Ctrl+Alt+- / =Smaller / larger text
    Ctrl+Alt+0Reset position, transparency and text size
    Ctrl+Shift+VRead answers aloud on / off (uses your default audio output)
    Ctrl+Shift+QQuit the app
  4. Free trial and your license key You get 30 minutes of live-session time within 14 days. When it ends, the overlay tells you. Open Settings from the tray icon, paste your license key (it starts with MC1.) into the License box and click Activate. Keys work offline, with no account or sign-in.

Troubleshooting

ProblemTry this
I can't see the overlayPress Ctrl+Shift+H (it may be hidden) or Ctrl+Alt+0 (reset position). It starts at the top-centre of your main screen.
I can't drag itIt is locked. Press Ctrl+Shift+M first, then drag the blue bar.
Nothing happens when people ask questionsCheck the Settings keys show green ticks, start a session (Ctrl+Shift+S), and make sure the sound is playing through your computer's output (headphones count). The tray icon shows the app is running.
It answers my own voice, or answers twiceIt only listens to your computer's sound output. If your speakers feed back into a microphone, use headphones.
Hotkeys don't workAnother program may use the same keys. Make sure only one copy of the app is running (check the tray), and quit with Ctrl+Shift+Q before starting again.
"No credit" / "key rejected"Open the service's console (Anthropic or Deepgram), check the key exists and the account has credit, create a new key if needed, and paste it in Settings.
The Windows warning has no "Run anyway"See the Smart App Control note in Part 1.
Prep pack says the resume has no readable textIt is probably a scanned image. Export your resume as a text-based PDF or a Word file and try again.

Uninstall

Open Settings > Apps > Installed apps, find Meeting Copilot and uninstall it. Your settings, prep packs and documents are kept so a reinstall picks up where you left off. To remove them too, delete the folder %APPDATA%\meeting-copilot (paste that into File Explorer's address bar).

Privacy at a glance

Audio from your call goes to Deepgram for transcription; question text and the few matching notes from your resume or documents go to Anthropic to write the answer. Your keys, resume, documents and prep packs stay on your computer. Get consent before recording or transcribing other people where the law requires it, and follow the rules of any interview or exam.
