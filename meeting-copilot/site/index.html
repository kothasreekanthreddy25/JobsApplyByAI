Questions

Can other people on the call see the overlay?

The overlay is excluded from screen sharing and recording by Windows, so people viewing your shared screen won't see it. That cannot protect against someone physically looking at your screen, a camera pointed at it, or software that captures at driver level.

Is it okay to use in a real interview?

That depends on the interviewer's rules. Many employers and exams prohibit outside help, and using it against the rules can cost you the offer. It is designed for practice and mock interviews, and for meetings and calls where assistance is allowed. Always get consent before transcribing other people's voices where the law requires it.

What do I need besides the app?

Two accounts that bill you directly for what you use: an Anthropic account (writes the answers) and a Deepgram account (turns speech into text). The install guide shows where to get each key. An OpenAI key is optional.

Where does my data go?

Audio of the meeting goes to Deepgram for transcription, and the question text goes to Anthropic to write the answer. Your resume and documents stay on your computer except for the text of a question and the few matching notes sent with it. Keys are stored encrypted on your computer. We don't receive your audio, transcripts or keys.

Windows warned me when I ran the installer. Is it safe?

The app is new and not yet code-signed, so Windows shows a one-time "protected your PC" screen for any unsigned installer. The install guide walks through it and shows how to verify the file's checksum against the one on this page.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

Not yet. Windows 10 and 11 only.
