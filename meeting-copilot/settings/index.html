Meeting Copilot

Private overlay that listens to your meeting or mock interview and answers questions. Only you can see it.

Stored encrypted on this computer only. Leave a box empty to keep the saved key.

2. Interview prep pack none yet

3. Reference documents

Put PDF, Word, text or Markdown files here (product notes, study material). Put files in a sub-folder named after a meeting keyword to use them only for that meeting.

4. Use it

It also starts by itself when Zoom, Teams, Meet or Webex shows a meeting.
Ctrl+Shift+Sstart / stop a session
Ctrl+Shift+Hshow / hide the overlay
Ctrl+Shift+Munlock to drag / resize, press again to lock
Ctrl+Alt + arrowsmove the overlay (add Shift to resize)
Ctrl+Alt+[ ] / - =background transparency / font size
Ctrl+Shift+Vread answers aloud on / off
Ctrl+Shift+Qquit

Recording or transcribing other people's voices needs their consent in many places, and many interviews and exams forbid outside help. Use this where you are allowed to.
